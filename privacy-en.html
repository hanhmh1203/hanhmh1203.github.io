Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 23, 2026

1. Introduction

MyLunar ("we") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use the MyLunar app.

2. Information We Collect

2.1. Personal Information (Optional)

  • Email & Name: When you sign in with Google for data backup
  • Family member data: Death anniversary information, names (stored on your device and Google Drive)

2.2. Device Information

  • Device ID: To display relevant advertisements
  • Crash logs: To improve the app (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • App usage data: To analyze performance

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide and improve app services
  • Back up and restore your data (via Google Drive)
  • Display relevant advertisements (Google AdMob)
  • Detect and fix bugs (Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Send notifications about important events

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Display advertisements (Privacy Policy)
  • Firebase (Google): Crashlytics, Remote Config, Cloud Messaging (Privacy Policy)
  • Google Drive: Data backup (only with your permission) (Privacy Policy)

5. Data Security

  • Encryption: Backup data is encrypted with AES-256
  • Local storage: Main data is stored on your device
  • HTTPS: All network connections are encrypted

6. Your Rights

  • Delete data: You can delete all data anytime in the app
  • Opt-out of ads: Go to Settings → Privacy → Advertising to disable personalized ads
  • Sign out: Disconnect Google Drive anytime

7. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years old.

8. Policy Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be notified through the app.
